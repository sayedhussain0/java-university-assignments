Blood sugar is considered normal if its rate is less than 140 and greater than 70, it is considered high its rate is greater than or equal to 140, and it is considered low its rate is less than or equal to 70.

Write a program that read form a file called "patient.txt" the data for unknown number of patients, each line in the file contain:

- First and the last name of the patient
- Age
- The rate of the blood sugar.

Print on Screen the following:

- The data of each patient (in one line) in addition to the string "Height", "Low" or "Normal" depending of the rate.
- The number of patients with normal blood sugar.
- The number of patients with high blood sugar.
- The number of patients with low blood sugar.
- The total number of patients in the file.
